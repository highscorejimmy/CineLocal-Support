Movie details and posters

CineLocal guesses a title from the file name and asks Apple's movie search for a match. A TMDB or OMDb key in Settings is optional.

📱

On-device playback

Apple's player for common formats, with VLCKit on the device when a file needs it. AirPlay and Picture in Picture use Apple's player.

🔒

Your videos stay put

Imported videos are copied into the app on your device. CineLocal has no account and does not upload your movies.

✈️

Offline after import

Once a movie and its poster are saved, you can watch them without an internet connection.

How it works

From file to featured film in a few taps.

1

Pick your videos

Use the + button to import a movie or folder from the iOS Files picker.

2

Look up a match

CineLocal reads a title and year out of the file name and asks Apple's iTunes Search service for public movie details and a poster. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is sent.

3

Watch on your device

Tap a poster and play. The video file stays in the app.
